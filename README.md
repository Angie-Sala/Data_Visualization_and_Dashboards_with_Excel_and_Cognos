# Car Sales & Profit Analysis
**📘 Project Overview**

This project was completed as part of my peer-graded final assignment in Coursera **Data Visualization and Dashboards with Excel and Cognos** course. The scenario involves working as a regional manager/data analyst at SwiftAuto Traders, a chain of car dealerships. The goal is to analyze car sales and profits per dealer and to design visual dashboards to help management make data-driven decisions.

**Tools Used:**
  * Microsoft Excel
  * IBM Cognos Analytics
  * Dataset: Modified subset from [IBM Accelerator Catalog - Automotive](https://community.ibm.com/accelerators/?context=analytics&type=Data&product=Cognos%20Analytics&industry=Automotive)


**📊Part 1 – Excel Visualizations:**
  * Quantity Sold by Dealer ID (Bar Chart)
  * Profit by Date and Model (Line Chart)
  * Profit by Year and Dealer ID (Column Chart, red)
  * Profit of Hudson Models by Dealer ID (Line Chart, green outline)


**📈Part 2 – Cognos Dashboards:**
  * Sales Dashboard: Profit, Quantity Sold, Avg Quantity, Quantity by Model, Profit by Dealer
  * Service Dashboard: Recalls per Model, Customer Sentiment, Monthly Sales vs Profit, Recalls Heatmap
